Our own product

Curvewright

Curvewright is a training app for cyclists. It estimates your FTP from your own ride history, then builds structured workouts and multi-week training plans that scale with it.

We design, build and run it ourselves, with AI agents as part of the everyday workflow. What we recommend to clients is what we already do on our own product.
